About

A bit about me

I'm Sayed Osman Sadat, a full stack developer. I started with a single HTML page and a lot of trial and error, and kept going until I could build the whole thing — the part people click and the part that keeps their data safe.

What I enjoy most is the middle of a project, when the pieces start connecting and a rough idea turns into something you can log into and use. I like clear code, small pull requests, and telling a client early when something will take longer than we hoped.

Outside of work I'm usually reading about system design or rebuilding something that already worked fine, just to see if I can do it better.
